## Summary of Allegation

An anonymous complainant alleges that between MAR 2026 and NOV 2026, the program
office accepted deliverables from a contractor without the independent technical
verification required by the contract's Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan.

## Preliminary Findings

F-1. Of 14 deliverables accepted in the period, **9 lack a signed verification
memorandum**. Contract clause H.12 requires verification prior to acceptance.

F-2. Invoices totaling **$1,340,000** were approved against the 9 unverified
deliverables. Payment was made in each case within 12 days of invoice receipt.

F-3. The Contracting Officer's Representative (COR) designation letter for the
period **expired on 30 APR 2026** and was not renewed until 14 JAN 2027. Nine
acceptances occurred during the lapse.

F-4. Two program staff reported that verbal pressure was applied to accelerate
acceptance ahead of the end of fiscal year. The OIG has not substantiated this.

## Recommendations

R-1. Suspend further acceptances under the contract pending verification of the
9 outstanding deliverables. Target completion: **45 days** from this memorandum.

R-2. Conduct a recovery review of the $1,340,000 and determine whether any
amount constitutes an improper payment under the Payment Integrity Information
Act. Report findings within **90 days**.

R-3. Re-issue COR designation letters for all active contracts in the program and
implement an expiration tracking control within **60 days**.

R-4. Refer F-4 to the agency's Office of Human Capital for inquiry. No criminal
referral is recommended at this time.
